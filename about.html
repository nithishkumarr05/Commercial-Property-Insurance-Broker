Our Story

About SecureBiz

We are an independent commercial insurance brokerage dedicated exclusively to protecting real estate investments, business assets, and operational continuity across the nation.

Who We Are

Independent Brokers.
Unbiased Protection.

Founded in 2008, SecureBiz was built on a simple premise: commercial property owners deserve an advocate, not a salesperson. As an independent brokerage, we are not tied to any single insurance carrier. This allows us to shop your risk across 40+ A-rated global underwriters to find the most comprehensive coverage at the most competitive premium.

We specialize in complex real estate portfolios—from single-tenant retail centers to multi-state industrial parks. Our team of former underwriters and certified risk managers understands how to structure policies that satisfy strict lender covenants, lease requirements, and board-level risk tolerances.

Our Mission

To provide institutional-grade property insurance solutions with the personal service of a boutique firm. We exist to protect commercial real estate investments from catastrophic loss, ensuring our clients can rebuild faster, recover revenues, and fulfill their fiduciary duties to investors and lenders.

Our Vision

To be the most trusted independent brokerage for commercial property risk in North America—known for our forensic valuation accuracy, proactive claims advocacy, and ability to structure complex layered towers for high-hazard coastal, seismic, and wildfire exposures.

The SecureBiz Advantage

Why Businesses Trust Us

Forensic Valuations

We don't guess replacement costs. We utilize proprietary valuation software to ensure your coinsurance penalties are eliminated.

Lender Compliance

We speak the language of your bank. We review all mortgagee clauses, loss payee endorsements, and loan covenants before binding.

24/7 Claims Advocacy

When a fire or storm hits, our claims team is on-site to document damage and advocate for maximum recovery—not the carrier's minimum.

Tailored Manuscripts

We don't accept off-the-shelf policies. We negotiate specific endorsements for flood sub-limits, ordinance of law, and equipment breakdown.
