Our Insurance Services

Comprehensive commercial property insurance solutions designed to protect your real estate assets, business operations, and financial stability against the unexpected.

Core Coverage

Commercial Property
Insurance

Protect your physical assets from fire, storms, vandalism, and other covered perils. Our property policies are structured on a full replacement cost basis, ensuring you can rebuild without financial shortfall, even in a catastrophic total loss scenario.

Building & Structures

Full replacement cost coverage for foundations, facades, roofs, and architectural details.

Tenant Improvements

Protection for build-outs, fixtures, and customizations you've installed in leased spaces.

Catastrophe Coverage

Dedicated wind, hail, flood, and earthquake sub-limits for high-hazard zones.

General Liability

Revenue Protection

Business Interruption
Insurance

Physical damage is only half the story. If a fire or storm forces your property to close, you still have mortgages, taxes, and payroll to meet. Business Interruption (BI) coverage replaces lost income and covers fixed expenses during the restoration period.

Loss of Rental Income

Replaces rent you would have collected while the property is uninhabitable.

Continuing Expenses

Covers fixed costs like mortgage interest, taxes, and key payroll during downtime.

Extended Period of Indemnity

Extends coverage until your business returns to pre-loss levels, not just when the building is rebuilt.
